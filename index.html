About the Festival

The Baltimore Afro-Food Festival is a joyful celebration of African, Caribbean, and Black diaspora food cultures thriving in Baltimore.

Come taste the soul of the diaspora — from smoky jollof and spicy suya to sweet plantains, injera with wat, Caribbean curry goat, soul food classics with an Afro twist, and innovative fusion creations by local chefs and vendors.

We bring together communities, celebrate our shared heritage, and honor the incredible culinary traditions that connect us across continents.
